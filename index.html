About the Masjid

Masjid-e-Tawheed Ahle Hadees serves the Muslim community of Umar Bagh, JP Nagar, Bangalore through Salah, Islamic education, Khutbahs, lectures and community programs.

Programs & Activities

Jumu'ah Khutbah

Weekly Friday Khutbah and Salah.

Islamic Lectures

Regular Deeni programs and beneficial talks.

Maktab

Islamic education and Qur'an learning for children.

Community Service

Charity and welfare activities for the community.
