About Masjid

Masjid-e-Tawheed Ahle Hadees is located in Umar Bagh, JP Nagar, Bangalore. The Masjid is dedicated to Salah, Qur'an, authentic Sunnah, Islamic education and serving the Muslim community.

Programs & Activities

Jumu'ah Khutbah

Weekly Jumu'ah Khutbah and Islamic reminders.

Islamic Lectures

Qur'an, Sunnah and beneficial Islamic lectures.

Maktab

Islamic education and Qur'an learning for children.

Community Service

Serving the community through beneficial activities.

Ramadan Programs

Special Ramadan activities, Taraweeh and Islamic reminders.

Seerah Programs

Learning about the life and guidance of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
